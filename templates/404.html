Chocolate Chip Cookies

Ingredients

  • 3 cups (390g) all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • ½ tsp baking powder
  • 1½ tsp salt
  • 1 cup (2 sticks) unsalted butter, softened
  • 1 cup (200g) granulated sugar
  • 1 cup (200g) brown sugar
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 large eggs, room temperature
  • 1½ cups chocolate chips

Directions

  1. Whisk the flour, baking soda, baking powder, and salt together in a medium bowl, set aside.
  2. In a large bowl, cream the butter, granulated, and brown sugars together. Beat in the vanilla extract and eggs.
  3. Fold in the dry ingredients, then add the chocolate chips. Cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes.
  4. Preheat oven to 350°F. Portion the dough into 2" balls, about 2 Tbsp each. Place on a parchemnt lined baking sheet and bake for 10-12 minutes.
